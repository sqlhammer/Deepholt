# Slice 015 — The long haul

> You come up from −3 with a full pack of iron and a lantern going out. You've done the climb
> twice already. This time you load a crate at the foot of the shaft, climb, and crank it up
> yourself, slowly, at the top. On the way down again you throw your spoil into the chute. When
> you lose your way in a tunnel you dug an hour ago, the map shows your ladders on every level,
> lined up, and you walk home by them.

*Proposed by Claude, 2026-11-10, after Derik accepted three larger slices (015 to 017, in
[NEXT.md](./NEXT.md)). **Agent mode** (Derik, 2026-11-10). **Started 2026-11-10:** forks decided
(D-215 to D-229). **Built** on `slice-015`, 2026-11-10 ([log](./log/015-the-long-haul.md)). **Closed and merged to `main`** 2026-11-11, at Derik's word, after his walk and the fixes it found (D-230 to D-236).*

## Where we are

- **Built:** the Surface, −1 and −2 generated by the seed, with caves; copper and tin on −2 only;
  digging, breaching, ladders (built, 4 timber), timber props, the support rule; a weight-limited
  pack and grid inventory; a hotbar of tools; dark vision, memory, and ore that reads grey
  unless lit by a brazier (006); the debug log (013) and play-test telemetry (014).
- **Not there:** level −3 has a recipe row (radius 102, "Old Terraces") but nothing in it. Nothing
  carries items between levels but the player. Light comes only from braziers. There is no map.
- **Why this slice:** the pre-alpha's question is whether hauling up a mountain is fun
  ([pre-alpha-scope §1](../docs/pre-alpha-scope.md)). The scope says three levels below the
  Surface is the minimum for the pain to bite, and today there are two. Pre-alpha criterion 1, a
  chute built unprompted before the 5th expedition, can't be measured with no chute.
- **PRs #9 to #13 are merged** (014b, the UAT fixes, D-211 to D-214, included).

## What should be true when you stop

By hand, unless marked:

1. **−3 exists and is worth going to.** You reach −3 from −2 (how: fork 1), it's larger than −2,
   dark, with caves and iron ore in veins; part of it is behind rubble you can't clear, and
   trying to says why, not "nothing happens".
2. ~~Iron is worth the climb~~ — moved to 016: iron needs bronze (D-217). In 015, a flint pick
   on iron ore is refused and says why.
3. **A chute:** build one over a hole; items dropped into it on one level land as drops on the
   level below. Spoil goes down without you.
4. **A hand winch and a crate:** fill a crate at the bottom, go up, crank at the top, and the
   crate comes up one level. It's slow on purpose. Any player can crank it, and two players can
   share one.
5. **Portable light:** carry a light (fork 5) and the ore around you shows its kind as you walk;
   put it away and it's grey again.
6. **The map** (M / pad View): one level at a time with the levels above and below ghosted under
   it; ladders, holes, chutes and winches drawn on every level they pass through; only what
   you've seen. Find your way back to −1 from a tunnel on −3 using only the map.
7. **A depth strip** on the HUD shows which level you're on and how many lie below.
8. **Telemetry** records chutes and winches built and used, and map opens, so criterion 1 can be
   counted per trip (D-198 promised the chute's events).
9. *Automated:* −3 generates and validates for many seeds; chute, winch and crate rules,
   including save and load mid-use; light's ore reveal; the map shows only remembered tiles;
   the telemetry events; no system assumes one player.

## Already settled

- **Weight-limited, no teleport** (rule [D-013](../docs/design-decisions.md)): the chute and
  winch are the answer to tedium, never a bigger pack.
- **About 15 minutes of hauling before the winch** ([D-029](../docs/design-decisions.md)): binds
  the winch's cost and the crate's size. Its levers, in order: winch cost, ore yield, carry
  capacity.
- **Down is cheap, up costs** ([vertical-logistics §1–2](../docs/systems/vertical-logistics-and-power.md)):
  the chute is early and cheap; the winch costs and is tedious on purpose.
- **Levels share one origin** ([world-and-generation §1](../docs/world-and-generation.md)): a
  chute's hole on −2 lands on the same (x, y) on −3; the map's anchors line up because of it.
- **Levels are open, regions are earned** ([D-012](../docs/design-decisions.md)); −3's gate is
  `CRUSHED`, not water ([pre-alpha-scope §2](../docs/pre-alpha-scope.md)), and must read as
  intriguing, not as a bug (criterion 6).
- **Iron is −3's ore** ([strata table](../docs/world-and-generation.md#2-strata)); −1 stays free
  of metal ([D-187](../docs/design-decisions.md)).
- **No ladder is placed by generation** ([D-185](../docs/design-decisions.md)).
- **Levels generate one ahead, on first descent** ([D-143](../docs/design-decisions.md)): −3
  exists once a player reaches −2, so a chute on −2 always has a level under it.
- **Light floods like the senses; a tile is lit or not** ([D-149](../docs/design-decisions.md));
  ore is unknown unless lit (006). A carried light is one more source in that flood.
- **Buildables take build slots** ([D-170](../docs/design-decisions.md)): chute, winch and crate
  join the build column.
- **The map** ([ui-ux §6](../docs/ui-ux-and-controls.md)): layer view, ghosted neighbors,
  vertical anchors at full brightness on every layer, the depth strip.
- **A trip** ([D-208](../docs/design-decisions.md)) is the "expedition" criterion 1 counts.
- **No system assumes one player** (rule [D-045](../docs/design-decisions.md)): any actor
  cranks, carries light, opens a map; the map shows what all players remember (D-145).

## Traps

### 1. −3 is out of reach today
Covered by fork 1. Whatever is chosen, a test must walk a fresh world's player from the Surface
to −3 with the starting kit plus what can be gathered, or the slice can pass its tests and fail
its first by-hand step.

### 2. Shared holes
A ladder, a chute and a winch all want a hole. If two can share one, every rule (climb, fall,
crank) has to know about the other; if they can't, building one over another's hole needs a
refusal. Decide in fork 4, and test both.

### 3. Where chuted items land
On the tile below the hole, which a breach left open. Drops there are picked up by a player who
steps on them (auto-pickup, D-160's rules), so a player at the bottom collects as they arrive.
A full tile, or a fixture there (a ladder's foot, a crate), needs a rule: spill to a neighbor.

### 4. A crate half way
Save and quit while the winch is cranking: the crate is on one level or the other, never
between. Collapse buries a crate and its contents, never destroys them (pre-alpha's
buried-not-destroyed rule).

### 5. Light that moves every step
A carried light reflows the light flood and re-uploads the state texture each step. −3 is 205
tiles across; measure in F3 and keep the flood local to the light's radius.

### 6. The map's cost and what it leaks
It draws levels the player isn't on, from their memory layers (D-147), at map scale. It must
never show a tile nobody has seen, or a cave's position, or a crushed region's insides.

### 7. Pad buttons
The pad is nearly full (NEXT carry-forward). View for the map moves quick save (fork 8); LT is
the capture combo's half (D-195).

### 8. Test time
The suite is ~41 s; a third generated level in every test world would push it past a minute.
Test worlds should build −3 only when a test needs it (NEXT carry-forward: a smaller recipe for
tests).

### 9. The jump on −1
Still open. More level changes this slice means more chances to see it. If the debug log
catches it, fix it in this slice.
